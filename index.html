Colours

The palettes below are for encoding data only — bars, lines, pie slices, map areas. Colours for chart elements (axes, gridlines, labels) are handled separately, later.

Every palette is shown at 7 colours so the full set is visible. In practice a categorical palette stays readable up to about 5–6 categories; beyond that, colours become hard to tell apart, including for colour-blind readers.

Current palette

The categorical palette from the EDA strategy — our starting point and baseline for comparison.

OKLCH

Built on a perceptual lightness staircase in OKLCH: same teal and pinkred anchors, with the light-pink and pure red swapped for a blue and a violet.

Pro: even lightness, more colour-blind-robust, works in black & white.

Con: slightly more muted — loses some of the current palette’s .
